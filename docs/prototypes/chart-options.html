Candle chart · three options

Same market data in all three. Tap a candle to select it (tap again to clear), switch the timeframe tabs, and use the light/dark toggle. In option 2, press and hold on the chart to place a new alert line. Every option stays in one Vico chart with the volume sharing the time axis (spec.md §2.3).

1 · Refined: today's chart, easier to read

  • Last price line: a dashed line at the current price with a tag on the axis, colored by the last candle.
  • Crosshair on the selected candle: vertical and horizontal lines, with the close tagged on the price axis and the time on the time axis.
  • Volume colored by candle direction, lighter.
  • Info line in two rows: time and the candle's own change (%), then O H L C V aligned in columns.
  • Vico: HorizontalLine decoration for the last price, a custom marker for the crosshair, a ColumnProvider per direction. Smallest change.

2 · Alert lines: the chart shows your alerts

  • Everything in option 1, plus each Active alert as a dashed line at its target, green for Above and red for Below, with the distance from the price.
  • An alert outside the visible range pins to the edge with an arrow, so the candles keep their scale.
  • Hold on the chart to place a line and create an alert at that price; it opens the existing sheet with the target filled.
  • Vico: one HorizontalLine per alert, a custom decoration for the pinned ones, a long-press handler on the host. Needs the Active alerts in HomeUiState (already read for the monitoring row).
  • Recommended: it ties the chart to what the app is for, price alerts.

3 · Focus: a line by default, candles on demand

  • A Line / Candles switch, persisted like the timeframe. The line shows the closes with a soft area fill and a dot at the last price.
  • High and low of the visible candles marked on the chart.
  • The selection shows a floating card next to the candle instead of the info line; the info line shows the visible range.
  • Volume shows only with candles.
  • Vico: LineCartesianLayer with an area fill, swapped with the candle layer; a custom marker for the card. The switch is a new DataStore key.
